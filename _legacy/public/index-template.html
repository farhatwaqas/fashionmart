FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I place an order?

Tap any “Order on WhatsApp” button. Your selected item is added to the message automatically, and we’ll confirm the details.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes. Send us your area on WhatsApp and we’ll confirm delivery options, timing, and charges.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Please contact us within 24 hours of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original condition; custom and hygiene-sensitive items may not be eligible.

Are the colors and prices final?

Colors can look slightly different on screens. Prices and stock are confirmed with you before your order is finalized.
